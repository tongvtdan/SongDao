# SongDao Vector Logo Handoff

## Source Files

- `songdao-logo-vector.svg` is the editable vector source for Affinity.
- `Logo-SongDao.png` is the original raster reference.

## Brand Decisions Encoded

- Chapel arch: Catholic identity, sacred space, quiet refuge.
- Integrated cross: centered structure of the journey, not a pasted symbol.
- Morning light: daily renewal and Today-screen promise.
- Path: primary differentiator, faith as something walked.
- Leaf: secondary growth cue only.

## Color Tokens

- Warm Off-white: `#FAF8F3`
- Chapel Green: `#1F7A64`
- Soft Green: `#E2F1EA`
- Sacred Gold: `#B8892E`
- Ink Shadow: `#1F2522`

## Affinity Workflow

1. Open `songdao-logo-vector.svg` in Affinity.
2. Keep the SVG groups as editable layer groups.
3. Save As `songdao-logo-vector.afdesign` in this folder.
4. Export app icon PNG at 1024 x 1024 with no rounded corners.

Small-size check: at 60 px, the chapel silhouette, cross, and path should still read. The leaf may disappear.
